3-Key Information

  • Capital: Al-Quds (Jerusalem)
  • Area: 27,027 km²
  • Official Language: Arabic
  • Religion: Islam (predominant) & Christianity
  • Main cities: Al-Quds, Gaza, Ramallah, Nablus

4-Brief History

  • The land of Palestine is considered an important region because of its strategic geographical location — situated in western Asia and close to the continents of Africa and Europe. This made it a place of conflict throughout history, both for its location and its religious significance, as it is regarded as a holy land by all major religions. Many prophets were sent there, and it is also where the miraculous Night Journey and Ascension (Al-Isra and Al-Mi‘raj) of the Prophet Mohamed.

    • During World War I, and specifically after the Balfour Declaration of 1917, the eyes of the Jews turned toward Palestine. Having been scattered across the world, they sought to establish a state — and unfortunately, this happened in 1948 with significant support from Britain and the United States.

    Today, the Palestinian people continue to strive for their independence. Despite setbacks, they fight and resist the occupation without the support of Arab countries. And despite all challenges, they remain steadfast, proud of their heritage, culture, and identity.
