Main Content

Since October 7, 2023, the Gaza Strip has been subjected to an unprecedented humanitarian catastrophe. The ongoing assault has resulted in devastating loss of life and massive destruction of infrastructure, affecting every aspect of civilian life in the besieged territory.

According to official sources, the death toll has exceeded 42,000 Palestinian martyrs, with more than 70% being women and children. Over 97,000 people have been injured, many with life-altering disabilities. Among the victims are more than 16,000 children and 11,000 women who lost their lives in this conflict.

The infrastructure damage is catastrophic. More than 60% of residential buildings have been destroyed or severely damaged, leaving over 1.9 million people displaced from their homes. The healthcare system has collapsed with 32 out of 36 hospitals rendered non-functional. Educational institutions have not been spared, with over 340 schools and universities damaged or destroyed.

The economic losses are estimated at over $18 billion, affecting water infrastructure, electricity networks, and communication systems. The blockade has created severe shortages of food, medicine, and fuel, leading to a humanitarian crisis of unprecedented scale. Basic necessities have become luxuries for the 2.3 million residents trapped in the Gaza Strip.

The international community has documented numerous violations of international humanitarian law, including attacks on civilian infrastructure, medical facilities, and places of worship. Despite global calls for ceasefire, the suffering of Gaza's population continues unabated.
